Review notes and keyboard shortcuts

Select a row, then P = possible feedback, R = rejected, U = unreviewed, T = toggle template; J/K = next/previous row. Click a filename for context.

Template means a recurring form, independently of your decision. Outgoing-only attachments are hidden by default; attachments also received remain visible. Unknown direction stays visible. Sender and date filters must match the same email occurrence.

Decisions save immediately to a separate SQLite database. Export includes all attachments and decisions, including hidden rows. No document extraction or classification runs here. Cached document evidence may contain quoted or outgoing text: it is context, not a new incoming message.
